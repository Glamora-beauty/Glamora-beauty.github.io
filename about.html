The Glamora Story

Navigating the world of international cosmetics and Korean skincare in Pakistan can be overwhelming. From verifying batch authenticity to finding your exact foundation undertone or skin-compatible serum, standard automated web shops often leave customers feeling uncertain.

Glamora was founded with a clear, customer-first objective: provide a refined, elegant beauty catalog showcasing original cosmetics from premier global houses, paired with a transparent WhatsApp concierge workflow.

Instead of relying on impersonal automated shopping carts or confusing online payment gateways, Glamora empowers you to curate your wishlist, select your exact desired shades, and coordinate directly with our beauty concierges via WhatsApp.
