Privacy Policy

At GLAMORA, we prioritize the privacy and security of our visitors. This Privacy Policy details the limited data collection practices of our static website and our direct WhatsApp ordering workflow.

1. Information Collected & WhatsApp Workflow

Glamora operates without mandatory account registration or invasive surveillance scripts. When you utilize our checkout form to compile an order, the contact details you enter (such as Full Name, WhatsApp Number, and Delivery Address) are formatted client-side inside your browser solely to assemble an order draft for WhatsApp.

This information is transmitted directly to WhatsApp when you initiate the communication. We do not sell, rent, or lease customer names or phone numbers to third-party marketing brokers.

2. Local Browser Storage (localStorage)

To deliver an intuitive shopping experience on GitHub Pages without requiring a server database or login cookies, Glamora utilizes modern browser localStorage for:

  • Shopping Bag: Storing selected products, quantities, shades, and customization notes.
  • Search History: Storing your recent search terms locally for quick discovery. You can clear this anytime with the "Clear History" button.
  • Anonymous Visitor ID: A randomly generated UUID string (e.g., glam_xxxxxxxx) used solely to prevent duplicate visitor counting in our footer analytics. This contains no personally identifiable data.

3. Live Visitor Statistics System

Our website displays aggregated visitor statistics in the footer (Total Visitors, Today's Visitors in Pakistan Standard Time UTC+5, Active Online Visitors, and Page Views).

This utilizes keyless CountAPI counters and optional anonymous Firebase Realtime Database presence channels. We never collect IP addresses, geolocation coordinates, or personal customer profiles for visitor counter purposes.

4. No Online Payment Gateway Storage

Glamora does not process credit cards, debit cards, or electronic wallet payments through this website. Consequently, no financial information, credit card numbers, or bank account credentials are ever requested or stored on this website.

5. Contact & Questions

For any questions or privacy inquiries, contact our official support concierge on WhatsApp at +92 318 3085519.
